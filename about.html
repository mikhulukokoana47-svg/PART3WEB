Our History

Established in 2017, Medigrow ZA began as a consultancy specialising in navigating the complex legislative and regulatory landscape of the medicinal cannabis sector, helping producers obtain the necessary licenses to operate legally. The company's trajectory shifted significantly under CEO Edgar Adams, who was motivated by the medicinal potential of cannabis and a personal loss.

This led to a bold new chapter: an R1 billion investment over five years to establish a major cannabis processing facility at the Coega Special Economic Zone in Gqeberha, Eastern Cape. The project aims to industrialise the sector, partnering with local rural farmers to create a sustainable and inclusive cannabis industry.

Our Mission

To position South Africa as a global leader in the medicinal cannabis industry by creating a sustainable, compliant, and inclusive value chain that fosters economic growth, creates jobs, and provides high-quality medicinal products for export.

Our Vision

To be the premier, vertically integrated cannabis company in Africa, driving rural development and economic transformation through strategic partnerships and world-class production.
