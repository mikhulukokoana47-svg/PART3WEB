About Medigrow ZA

Established in 2017, Medigrow ZA is a pioneering South African company that has emerged as a transformative force in the nation's medicinal cannabis industry. Under the visionary leadership of CEO Edgar Adams, the company has embarked on an ambitious R1 billion investment over five years to establish a state-of-the-art cannabis processing facility at the Coega Special Economic Zone in Gqeberha, Eastern Cape.

Our mission is to position South Africa as a global leader in the medicinal cannabis industry by creating a sustainable, compliant, and inclusive value chain that fosters economic growth and provides high-quality medicinal products for international export.
